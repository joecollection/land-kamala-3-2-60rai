Balanced Due Diligence

Upside Catalysts
Kamala–Patong road tunnel (planned) — once completed, dramatically shortens commute and boosts land values along this corridor
2nd Phuket International Airport in planning (+12.5M annual passengers) — step-change in tourism volume
Strong USD/EUR/GBP versus THB makes this an exceptional value buy for foreign investors in 2026
Structural land scarcity on west coast — finite developable ridgeline plots; this may be among the last
Global UHNWi wealth migration to lifestyle destinations — Phuket direct beneficiary (60%+ foreign buyers)
Risk Considerations
Hillside zoning regulations — height limits and coverage caps apply; professional feasibility study required pre-acquisition
Short-term rental regulation tightening — hotel license or SPV structure may be required for revenue operations
Construction cost inflation (+8–12% in 2025) — capex projections require contingency buffer of 15–20%
Foreign ownership restrictions — land must be held via Thai juristic entity or long-term leasehold structure
Monsoon seasonality (May–Oct) — revenue planning must account for lower occupancy in off-peak months
