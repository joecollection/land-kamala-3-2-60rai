12 · Why This Land

The Deal

A freehold, sea-view hillside plot where a ten-villa estate is the one scenario the numbers support.

The plot offers 5,840 m² of freehold land with 75 m frontage among established luxury neighbours. The seller's concept projects ฿450M revenue and a 42% margin.

Our adjusted base case shows ฿400M GDV, ฿106M profit and a 26.5% margin, with a residual land value of ฿86M. That is a thin cushion over the ask, so the price is fair rather than cheap.

Before committing, confirm FAR and height, obtain a cost estimate, and test the villa price against closed sales. The upside is real if planning allows more GFA.
